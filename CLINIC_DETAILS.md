### 7. Clinical Services & Surgical Procedures

1. **Robotic Knee Replacement:** Sub-millimeter robotic precision, custom bone-sparing resection, faster pain-free recovery, and maximum implant longevity.
2. **Total Knee Replacement (TKR):** Restores stability and natural range of motion for severe osteoarthritis and joint degeneration.
3. **Hip Replacement Surgery (Total & Partial):** Advanced pain relief and restored mobility using state-of-the-art dual-mobility and ceramic bearings.
4. **Shoulder Replacement Surgery (Reverse & Total):** Alleviates debilitating shoulder arthritis and massive rotator cuff arthropathy.
5. **Knee Arthroscopy (Keyhole Surgery):** Minimally invasive repair for meniscus tears, loose bodies, cartilage defects, and synovial disorders.
6. **ACL & Ligament Reconstruction:** Advanced hamstring/patellar tendon autografts and remnant-preserving techniques for athletes.
7. **Shoulder Arthroscopy & Rotator Cuff Repair:** Keyhole repair for recurrent dislocations (Bankart/Latarjet), labral tears, and tendon tears.
8. **Complex Trauma & Fracture Care:** 24/7 emergency fixation of comminuted, peri-articular, and failed fixation fractures.
9. **Sports Injury & Joint Preservation:** High Tibial Osteotomy (HTO), cartilage restoration, and IOC-certified rehabilitation programs.
